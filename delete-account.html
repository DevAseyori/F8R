Delete your F8r account

F8r · Last updated: September 19, 2026

This page explains how to delete your F8r account, or your guest's data, and what happens to your data afterwards. It applies to the Android app F8r (package life.f8r.game) published on Google Play by Wavori.

Two kinds of players. F8r creates a guest for you on our server the first time you play; a guest has no name and no email address. If you chose Create account on the account screen, that guest became a registered account with your email address. Both can be deleted the same way inside the game.

Delete inside the game (guest and registered account)

  1. Open F8r and go to the main menu.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my data if you play as a guest, or Delete account if you are signed in with an email address. The button is at the bottom of the screen, in red.
  4. Read the warning and tap Delete. Tap Keep to change your mind; nothing is sent until you tap Delete.
  5. The screen confirms with “Deleted. Nothing of yours is left on the server, and you are a fresh guest on this device.” You can keep playing as a new guest or uninstall the app.

Deletion needs an internet connection; if the server cannot be reached the screen says so and nothing is deleted. Try again later.

Delete by email (registered account only)

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email from the address of your account to devaseyori@gmail.com with the subject Delete my F8r account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply. We need the email to come from the account's address so that nobody else can delete your account.

A guest cannot be deleted by email, because nothing except the app on your device identifies a guest to us. Delete a guest from inside the game before uninstalling. A guest that has not opened the game for one year is removed automatically, together with everything stored under it.

What is deleted

Deleting removes, at once and for good, everything stored under your user ID on our server:

  • your current run (team, shop, gold, hearts, trophies, round);
  • your last fights (up to 20);
  • any divergence reports the app sent for your runs;
  • the account itself: your user ID, the email address and password hash of a registered account, and all sessions, so every device is signed out.

The game on your device then continues as a fresh guest with nothing in it. Deleting cannot be undone; there is no grace period during which the account could be restored.

What is kept, and for how long

DataAfter deletion
Account, run, fights, divergence reportsGone immediately.
Technical logs at our backend provider (request path, time, status, IP address of account events)Expire on their own, no later than 7 days after they were written. They are not tied to your account any more once it is gone.
Backups at our backend providerDaily database backups, where enabled, are kept for 7 days; a deleted row can survive in one for up to 7 days and is never restored for an individual account.
Your email to usKept only as long as needed to handle the request, then deleted.
Settings and the age confirmation on your phoneStay on your device until you uninstall the app; they are never sent to us.
