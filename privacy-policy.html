Privacy Policy

F8r · September 19, 2026 · Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what F8r (“F8r”, “the game”, “we”, “us”) does with your data. F8r is an auto battler for Android in which you build a team of martial artists and watch it fight. The game is offered by the controller named under Controller.

The short version: when you first open F8r we ask you to confirm that you are 13 or older; after that the game creates a guest account for you on our server, which is how your run is saved. You can turn that guest into an account with an email address and password, and you can delete the guest or the account, with everything stored under it, from inside the game. We show no ads, run no analytics and use no crash reporting. We do not sell data.

2. Age requirement

F8r is for players aged 13 and older. The first time you open the game, before anything is sent to our server, we ask you to confirm that you are at least 13. Only your confirmation is stored, and only on your device; we do not store your age and we do not verify what you tell us. Without the confirmation the game does not continue and no account is created.

3. What data we process, and why

3.1 Your guest account

F8r has no offline save. To keep your run, the game creates an anonymous user (a “guest”) on our server the first time it starts after the age confirmation, and signs that guest in on every later start. A guest has no name and no email address. What exists about a guest is:

  • a random user ID, the time it was created and the time it was last used;
  • a session, which our backend provider records with the IP address the session was opened from, the name and version of your operating system, the brand and model of your device, the name and version of the app, and the country derived from the IP address (see Appwrite Cloud for the source of this list).

Purpose and legal basis: providing the game and saving your run (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract); the session data serves the security of the account and of our service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in preventing abuse).

3.2 Your registered account

If you choose Create account on the account screen, your guest becomes an account with the email address and password you enter. The user ID stays the same, so everything stored under the guest becomes the account's. The password is stored only as a salted hash (Argon2); we cannot read it. Our provider additionally derives from the address whether it belongs to a free, disposable or corporate email service and whether the password appears in known breaches; we do not use these flags.

We use the email address only to sign you in and to answer a request you send us from it. We send no newsletters and no notifications. Email verification and password recovery are not offered at the moment; if you lose the password, you can only delete the account and start over.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

3.3 Game data

Everything you do in the game is stored under your user ID on our server, because the server, not the phone, decides the outcome of every fight. This is:

DataWhat it contains
Your runRound, gold, hearts, trophies, the team, the shop shelf, the chosen sets and backdrop, internal seeds and version counters, and when it was last updated.
Your last fightsBoth teams as they entered the fight, the opponent (a generated team, never another player's data), the seed, the outcome, hearts lost, trophies gained, and the time. Only the 20 newest fights are kept.
Divergence reportsIf the game on your phone and the server disagree about the result of your shop actions, the app sends the actions and both results to us so we can find the bug. The report holds the run ID, version counters, the actions, and the fields that differ. It contains no text you wrote and nothing about your device.

There are no leaderboards, no matchmaking against other players, no chat and no usernames. Nothing of yours is shown to other players.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the run and the fights; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for the divergence reports (our legitimate interest in a game that plays the same on the phone and on the server).

3.4 Technical logs of our backend

Every request the game makes to our server function is recorded by our provider as an execution log: the method and path of the request, the time, the duration, the result status and some request headers. Request and response bodies are not logged. Our provider also keeps an audit log of account events (creation, sign-in, deletion) with the user, the event, the IP address, the location derived from it and the time. We read these logs only to find and fix errors and to investigate abuse.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a service that works and is not abused).

3.5 What we do not collect

F8r shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking SDK, sends no crash reports, uses no push notifications, and asks for no permissions beyond network access. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health or payment data. There are no in-app purchases. Should any of this change, this policy and the Data safety section on Google Play will be updated before the change ships.

3.6 Google Play

You install F8r through Google Play. What Google collects when you do so (for example your Google account, device information and installation statistics) is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; we receive only aggregate, anonymous statistics from the Play Console.

4. Data that stays on your device

The app keeps the following on your phone only, in its private storage, and never sends it to us: your settings (volume, haptics, language, reduced motion), your age confirmation, a copy of your current run and your last fights so the game can show them while the server is unreachable, and the session token that signs your guest or account in. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Uninstalling does not delete your account on the server; see Deleting your account.

5. Where the data is stored (Appwrite Cloud)

Our backend runs on Appwrite Cloud, a service of Appwrite Code Ltd. (privacy policy). Appwrite processes your data on our behalf as a processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). We use the Frankfurt (FRA) region: all data at rest, including database rows, sessions, logs and backups, stays in Germany; Appwrite states that regions are isolated and data remains within the selected region. Appwrite's own sub-processors are listed in its data processing agreement.

6. Sharing

We share your data with nobody except Appwrite as described above. We disclose data to authorities only when a valid legal request obliges us to (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. If F8r were ever transferred to another operator, you would be informed beforehand.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your guest or your registered account, and with it everything stored under it on our server, from inside the game: open the menu, choose Account, then Delete my data (as a guest) or Delete account (with an account), and confirm with Delete. The server then deletes your run, your fights and your divergence reports, and finally the account itself with its email address, password hash and sessions. This happens at once and cannot be undone. The game then continues with a fresh, empty guest on your device.

If you have a registered account and no longer have the app, email us from the address of the account at devaseyori@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my F8r account”; we delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. A guest can only be deleted from the game, because nothing except the app on your device identifies a guest to us; a guest that has not been used for a year is removed by our clean-up.

Step-by-step instructions, and what is kept for how long afterwards, are on our account deletion page.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are stored as salted Argon2 hashes. Access to the server's tables is restricted to our server function and to us; the app itself can only reach the function, and the function only ever answers the signed-in user about their own data. Our provider holds a SOC 2 Type II attestation. No system is perfectly secure, and we will inform you and the supervisory authority as the GDPR requires should a breach affect your data.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have it rectified (Art. 16), erased (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing that rests on our legitimate interests (Art. 21). We make no automated decisions about you. To exercise a right, email devaseyori@gmail.com; for a registered account, write from the account's address so we can match the request to you. Erasure you can do yourself at any time in the game, see above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your residence. A list of the German supervisory authorities is published by the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection at bfdi.bund.de.

Your consent is not the basis of any processing described here, so there is nothing to withdraw; if we ever ask for consent (for example for advertising), you will be able to withdraw it in the game at any time.

11. Children

F8r is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly process their data; the age confirmation at first start is there to keep them out. Should we learn that a child under 13 is playing, we delete the account and everything under it. Parents or guardians who believe this has happened can write to devaseyori@gmail.com.
