1. Age Requirement

F8R is intended for use by people aged 13 and older. By creating an account or using the App, you represent that you are at least 13 years old. We do not currently ask users for their age or date of birth, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. See Children's Privacy below for more detail.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an F8R account, we collect the email address, username, and password you provide. Your password is stored in encrypted (hashed) form — we cannot see or recover your actual password.

Gameplay and Usage Data

To run matchmaking, leaderboards, and your in-game progress, we collect data generated as you play, such as match results, win/loss record, leaderboard rank, in-game progress, and general app usage (features used, session activity).

Advertising Identifiers

F8R shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may access device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. See Third-Party Services for details.

Diagnostic and Crash Data

We use a crash reporting and performance monitoring tool (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry) to find and fix bugs. This may automatically collect technical data such as device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, and crash/error logs.

Purchases

F8R does not currently offer in-app purchases, and we do not collect any payment or purchase data. If we introduce in-app purchases in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and our Play Console Data safety information before doing so.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data, and we do not collect health or financial information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and let you sign in across sessions
  • To operate core gameplay features: matchmaking, battles, leaderboards, and progress saving
  • To display ads and measure ad performance
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve app performance and stability
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, cheating, or abuse
  • To communicate with you about your account or respond to support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for purposes beyond what's needed to run and improve F8R and to communicate with you, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: With third parties that help us operate F8R, such as our advertising provider (Google AdMob), crash/diagnostics provider, and cloud hosting/backend infrastructure providers — only to the extent needed for them to perform their function.
  • Other players: Your username and gameplay/leaderboard data (such as rank or win record) may be visible to other players as part of normal gameplay.
  • Legal reasons: If required to comply with a valid legal request, protect our rights, or prevent fraud or harm.
  • Business transfers: If F8R is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you as required by law.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so we can provide the App's features. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data immediately. Limited data may remain briefly in encrypted backup systems until those backups naturally cycle out, and we may retain minimal information where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit using HTTPS/TLS and storing passwords in hashed form. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using industry-standard practices.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and, in some regions, to opt out of certain data practices. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at devaseyori@gmail.com.

We do not sell personal data, so there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. You can also control ad personalization at the device level through your Android device's Ads settings.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your F8R account and associated data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In the App: Go to your account or settings screen and select the option to delete your account.
  • Outside the App: Email devaseyori@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my F8R account," and we will delete your account and data.

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete your associated personal data — we do not just freeze or deactivate your account — subject to the limited retention exceptions described in Data Retention above.

10. Children's Privacy

F8R is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at devaseyori@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

11. International Users

F8R may be used by people around the world, and our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required by applicable law, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as F8R adds new features (such as in-app purchases). We will update the "Last Updated" date above when we do, and, where changes are significant, we will provide additional notice in the App.
